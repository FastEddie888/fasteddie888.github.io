Hey, I'm Eddie.

Figma Enthusiast / Lean UX Practitioner / B2B Collaborator

Senior Product Designer with 9+ years of experience spanning onboarding, shopping, and decision-making experiences within credit card technology. Currently a Sr. Product Designer at Wells Fargo, specializing in crafting user-centered digital experiences. Prior to design, I worked as a software engineer focused on front-end application development, giving me a unique perspective on bridging design and engineering.

I began my career as a software developer after studying Computer Science, but transitioned into product design after discovering my passion for creating meaningful user experiences. My technical foundation enables me to effectively translate complex business requirements and customer needs into practical, scalable design solutions that align with both user expectations and engineering realities.

In addition to traditional design practices, I leverage modern AI tools such as ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, and Devin AI to accelerate ideation, streamline research synthesis, and translate design concepts into usable UI specifications and implementation-ready artifacts for engineering teams. By integrating AI into the product design process, I help bridge the gap between vision and execution, enabling faster collaboration, clearer handoffs, and more efficient product delivery.

My design philosophy centers on purposeful, strategic creativity. I believe impactful design emerges from deep user research, rigorous validation, collaborative teamwork, and thoughtful use of emerging technologies. This approach ensures solutions not only achieve business objectives but also deliver meaningful value to users while remaining feasible and efficient to build.
